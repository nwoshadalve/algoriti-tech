# Online deployment model

## 1. Customer and deployment

- For single-center and multi-branch diagnostic organizations.
- Algoriti Tech hosts and maintains the system; staff access it through browsers.
- Internet is required for daily use. Offline operation is not included.
- Branches operate under one organization account with branch-based permissions.
- Proposed branch policy: no fixed plan-based branch limit; charge per branch and confirm deployment capacity before purchase.

## 2. Subscription

- One plan with all standard features; no feature tiers.
- Monthly and annual subscribers receive the same features.
- The base subscription includes the first branch; additional branches receive discounted pricing under the same organization agreement.
- A branch is a separate operating location, not a counter or department. Separate businesses require separate base plans.
- Include hosting, backups, standard updates, future standard features, and defined support while subscribed.
- Standard software upgrades have no separate fee; customer-specific work and usage-based services are separate.
- Patient data belongs to the customer.

## 4. Support and cancellation

- Define support hours, limits, and service availability commitments before sale.
- Algoriti Tech manages hosting, security, backups, and recovery.
- Define payment grace periods, cancellation, data export, and retention terms before launch.
- Application access ends after the agreed grace period when the subscription expires. Export and retention follow the agreed terms; there is no permanent local license.
- Subscription does not include source-code ownership or unlimited customization.
